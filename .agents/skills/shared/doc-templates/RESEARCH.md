# Research note template

One question per file. Follow the rules in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md).

```md
# <The question, as a title>

Sources and provenance: <repo + SHA, doc URLs + date read>
Feeds: <the decision or document this answers for, or "standalone">

## Summary

<3 to 8 lines. The answer, with confidence tiers.>

## Findings

<One section per sub-question. Each claim tagged and cited. Short quotes where the wording is the evidence.>

## What we don't know

<Specific gaps: what was searched, where, for what.>

## Sources consulted

<One line per source category, including "not searched" with the reason.>
```
